## 3. Architecture & Conformance Check

- **[ADR 006 (Component Dependency DAG)](../decisions/006_architectural_dag_and_conformance.md)**: `ast` depends only on `diagnostic` and `syntax`; `semantic::type_annotations` depends on `ast::type_annotations` and `syntax`; `rules::*` depend on `ast`, `semantic`, `diagnostic`, `rule_declaration`, and `syntax`. Verified by `cargo test --test architecture_conformance`.
- **[ADR 011 (Colocated Abstraction Ordering)](../decisions/011_colocated_abstraction_ordering.md)**: Every new and modified module places public entrypoints and types above private helpers (`callee_after_caller`), keeps sibling pipelines in execution order, and keeps `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` at the bottom.
- **[ADR 012 (Module Placement by Abstraction)](../decisions/012_module_placement_by_abstraction.md)**:
  - Language-neutral structural representations (`TypeAnnotationSite`, `TypeAnnotationRoot`, `TypeAnnotationCandidate`, `TypeShape`, `collect_type_annotation_sites`, `collect_type_annotation_candidates`, `positional_tuple_parameter_annotations`, `positional_tuple_attribute_annotations`) live in [src/code_lint/ast/type_annotations.rs](../../../src/code_lint/ast/type_annotations.rs).
  - Python-specific and Rust-specific type annotation extractors live in [src/code_lint/ast/python/annotations.rs](../../../src/code_lint/ast/python/annotations.rs) and [src/code_lint/ast/rust/annotations.rs](../../../src/code_lint/ast/rust/annotations.rs).
  - Pattern grammar parsing, `WrittenSymbol` / `STD_TYPE_CONSTRUCTORS` resolution, and structural matching live in [src/code_lint/semantic/type_annotations.rs](../../../src/code_lint/semantic/type_annotations.rs).
  - Rule declarations, option wiring, and diagnostic formatting live in [src/code_lint/rules/positional_tuple_parameter.rs](../../../src/code_lint/rules/positional_tuple_parameter.rs), [src/code_lint/rules/positional_tuple_attribute.rs](../../../src/code_lint/rules/positional_tuple_attribute.rs), and [src/code_lint/rules/adhoc_type_annotation.rs](../../../src/code_lint/rules/adhoc_type_annotation.rs).
